# player-board

- **Question**: how should the player boards look so they scale to any screen, stay readable, always show the essentials, open details on hover/click in the same style, and grow for the Blighted Reach campaign (flagship board, Fate, titles, favours)?
- **How to run**: `npm run dev`, then `/prototypes/player-board/index.html#a` (scenes `#a` to `#e`; add `?w=1366&h=768` to see a smaller screen).
- **Status**: mockups for the owner's review (2026-11-24).
- **Scenes**:
  - A: the physical board, faithful: six slots with their keys, cities on the slots, +2/+3 spaces, the five Prelude actions with Outrage, trophies and captives; the played card beside the board, the hand held free of any frame.
  - B: a compact band for every seat (essentials only); the full board opens in a hover window in the same style.
  - C: Blighted Reach: the same board with a second tier (flagship board, Fate, titles, favours) that folds away.
  - D: the resource rules as interactions: gaining with full slots, a city coming back, spending in the Prelude, Outrage.
  - E: the owner's pick, A + B (2026-11-24): own board faithful (A), rivals as bands (B); the Court bar on top (draw pile, the cards to take with agents, discard); ambitions as the printed column, plus markers and live counts; own Court bottom right, cards overlapping only when they do not fit.
- **Findings**: the owner chose A + B and asked for the Court bar, the own Court bottom right and a printed-like ambitions column (scene E).
